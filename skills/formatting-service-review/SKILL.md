# Formatting for service review

The output rules already apply to every answer and are carried in the standing
instructions: lead with the figure, table for comparison and prose for
interpretation, name the table and the filter, state the caveat, answer in the
language asked.

Nothing here changes them, and no rule is restated — a second copy would be a
weaker one that drifts.

Two constraints are worth naming because a request for a *table* can make them
feel negotiable. They are not, and both live in the standing instructions:
individuals are reported in aggregate and by rank rather than by name, and no
figure may appear that neither the data nor the wiki supplies. A presentation
request does not relax either one.
