# Escalation matrix

| Breach severity | Notified | Within |
|---|---|---|
| P1 breach | Service owner and duty manager | 1 working hour |
| P2 breach | Service owner | 1 working day |
| P3 / P4 breach | Reported in the weekly service review | Weekly |

Escalation is triggered on the breach itself, not on a trend. Trend
questions are handled in the monthly service review.
